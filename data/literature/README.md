# Published starting tables and modified outputs

The three inputs are transcribed from the final lookup tables in these two articles:

- Alejandro Freyre-Echevarría et al., *An External Parameter Independent Novel Cost Function for Evolving Bijective Substitution-Boxes*, Symmetry 12(11), 1896 (2020), [doi:10.3390/sym12111896](https://doi.org/10.3390/sym12111896), Appendix A, S8, PDF page 13.
- Alexandr Kuznetsov et al., *Optimizing Hill Climbing Algorithm for S-Boxes Generation*, Electronics 12(10), 2338 (2023), [doi:10.3390/electronics12102338](https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics12102338), Appendix A, S-box2 and S-box3, PDF page 15.

Both articles are distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The `inputs/` tables preserve the published values. The `final/` tables are modifications obtained by the explicitly recorded transpositions in `sources-and-witnesses.json`; no change of source authorship is implied.

All inputs have standard vectorial NL 104, minimum degree 6, maximum degree 7 and differential uniformity 8. The improved outputs retain NL 104 and differential uniformity 8 and have all 255 nonzero components of degree 7. These are three examples from two papers, not three independently developed construction methods.
